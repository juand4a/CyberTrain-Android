CyberTrain v8

- Replaced the generic ToneGenerator alert with the supplied GYM alarm MP3.
- The supplied alarm is played for exactly 7 seconds and then stopped.
- No AudioFocus is requested, so CyberTrain does not ask the user's music player to pause.
- Added AlarmManager fallback plus a foreground-service Handler based on absolute restEndsAt.
- Rest timer remains anchored to an absolute timestamp instead of decrementing a counter.
- Added RestTimerReceiver to recover the rest timer when the app UI is closed or the service is recreated.
- Foreground service remains START_STICKY and rehydrates the active session from DataStore.
- Added exact-alarm permission with fallback to setAndAllowWhileIdle when exact alarms are not available.
- Kept the cyberpunk expanded notification artwork.
- Test alert button now uses the same 7-second MP3.
- Force Stop remains an Android system exception: a force-stopped app cannot restart its service/alarm until the user opens it again.
